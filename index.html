Privacy Policy

A dramatic exit. An exceptionally undramatic approach to your privacy.

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Self Destruct does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not request your name, email address, account credentials, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or other personal information. It does not read your files or the list of installed applications.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, telemetry, or tracking services.

2. Permissions and internet access

Self Destruct requests no Android permissions and does not request internet access. Its countdowns and animations run locally on your device.

3. Data storage

The app does not save user-generated content or create user accounts. Any in-app experience counters exist only during the current app session and are not transmitted or stored for later use.

4. How the self-destruct feature works

Practice Run plays a simulated countdown and ending. It does not request uninstallation or delete anything.

The Real Deal plays the countdown and then asks Android to display its official uninstall confirmation for Self Destruct itself. You decide whether to approve or cancel. The app does not silently uninstall itself, remove other apps, or erase your personal files.

Android is responsible for the system uninstall operation if you confirm it.

5. Cost and third parties

The app is free, with no advertisements, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or paid features. It does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

6. Changes to this policy

If the app's functionality or privacy practices change, this page will be updated to reflect those changes, with a revised effective date when appropriate.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact details shown on the Self Destruct Google Play listing when available, or visit the Frogdadday GitHub profile.

In short: No data collection. No tracking. No surprises. Unless you press the button.
